What Shell City is

Clock in. Get paid.
Build a life.

A living city on Roblox where players do the work, run the shops and spend the money. You walk off the bus with nothing, and the city looks after you while you find your feet.

Arrive

Water at the fountain, soup at the Soup Kitchen and a bench to rest on are free, forever. Grab a free bike and look around.

Work

Pick a job off the board: pump the fountain, flip burgers, run a market stall, work the docks. Each one is its own short minigame.

Get paid

Every task pays its posted price by the stars you earn, out of the business's wage pot. Nothing is printed to pay you.

Live

Eat, rent a stall, grow a garden, dance with a crowd at the Drop, and save up for a place of your own in the Heights.

Businesses

Run the shop,
not just the till.

Some businesses belong to the city. Others can be claimed by a player, and every market stall can be rented by the day.

Claim a business

The Burger Stand, Harbour Canteen, Fish Market, Pine & Brass, the Strip Soda Bar and the Neon Arcade can each be claimed by a player. The owner chooses how much of the takings goes to the wage pot and how much to the till.

Rent a stall

24 stalls on Market Street, from pierogi and pho to bubble tea and churros. Rent one for a day, set your own price within its range, and hire a cook and a register.

The free tier

The Soup Kitchen, the Fountain and the Park Bench are always open and always free. Their wage pots also get every withdrawal fee, so they pay the people who keep them running.

The city economy

Spent here,
paid here.

Inside Shell City, $SHELL is simply money: what you earn, spend and save. Every price, fee and wage follows the same few rules.

  1. No printed wagesWages come out of each business's wage pot, filled by what players spend there and a slow hourly release from the city's payroll fund. No coin is minted to pay anyone.
  2. Real market pricesPrices come from the live market on Solana, the blockchain $SHELL runs on. The game never makes one up.
  3. No wallet connect, no keysYou never connect a wallet or see a private key. Withdraw to any Solana address when you want to.
  4. Free means freeSoup, water and a bench always work, just slowly. Nobody pays to play.
  5. No hidden costsEvery price, fee and loss is shown at the moment you decide.

Questions

Good to know

When can I play?

Now. Shell City is open on Roblox. Follow @ShellCityFun for new districts, jobs and events.

Is it free?

Yes. Shell City is free to play on Roblox. Food, water and rest are free forever, and your first wage comes from working, not paying.

Do I need crypto to play?

No. You never need a wallet to play, work, earn or spend. $SHELL is just the money inside the city. A wallet only matters if you want to take money out.

What is $SHELL?

$SHELL is a coin on Solana, a public blockchain. In the game it is the money every wage, price and purchase is counted in. The whole money system has been run end to end on Solana's free test network, with public transactions: see Proof & details.

Where did $SHELL launch?

On pump.fun on __DATE__, from inside Shell City. The launch record is at shellcity.fun/proof.

What are city coins?

Coins for the city's businesses, each bought and sold for $SHELL. Later, players will be able to launch their own from the trading terminal. Follow @ShellCityFun for the day city coins open.

How do I take money out?

Earned $SHELL can leave once your Street Cred reaches 100. You build that up by playing over a few days: paid shifts, a meal or rent, a delivery or repair. You send it to any Solana address you choose, and the fee is shown before you confirm. The 2,500 $SHELL start grant is spendable in the game but never withdrawable.

Can I lose money?

Yes. Token prices can fall as well as rise, and trading city coins can lose money. Prices, fees and losses are always shown before you decide. Earnings are never guaranteed, and nothing on this site is financial advice.

Will you ever ask for my seed phrase?

Never. Shell City never asks for a seed phrase or private key, never asks you to connect a wallet, and never messages you first about money. Anyone who does is not us.

Who is making it?

A small independent studio. Shell City is not made, sponsored or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
