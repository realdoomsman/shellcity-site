A day in the city

Clock in. Get paid.
Build a life.

No grind wall and no paywall. You walk off the bus with nothing, and the city looks after you while you find your feet.

Arrive

Water at the fountain, soup at the Soup Kitchen and a bench to rest on are free, forever. Grab a free bike and look around.

Work

Pick a job off the board: pump the fountain, flip burgers, run a market stall, work the docks. Each one is its own short minigame.

Get paid

Every task pays its posted price by the stars you earn. The money comes from the business's wage pot, filled by what players spend there.

Live

Eat, rent a stall, grow a garden, dance with a crowd at the Drop, and save up for a place of your own in the Heights.

$SHELL

The city where
the money is real.

$SHELL is a Solana token. Inside Shell City it is simply money: what you earn, spend and save. Outside, it is yours to keep.

  1. No printed wagesEvery wage comes out of what players spent at that business. The only coin the game ever creates is a one-time 2,500 $SHELL start grant.
  2. One price, on chainEvery price comes from an on-chain pool. The game never makes one up.
  3. No wallet connect, no keysYou never connect a wallet or see a private key. Withdraw to any Solana address when you want to.
  4. Free means freeSoup, water and a bench always work, just slowly. Nobody pays to play.
  5. No hidden costsEvery price, fee and loss is shown at the moment you decide.
85%to the business, which pays its workers

Questions

Good to know

When can I play?

Soon. Shell City is finished and in testing, and we will announce the opening on X, TikTok and Instagram first. Follow @ShellCityFun so you don't miss it.

Is it free?

Yes. Shell City is free to play on Roblox. Food, water and rest are free forever, and your first wage comes from working, not paying.

Do I need crypto to play?

No. You never need a wallet to play, work, earn or spend. $SHELL is just the money inside the city. A wallet only matters if you want to take money out.

What is $SHELL?

$SHELL is a token on Solana. In the game it is the money every wage, price and purchase is counted in. The whole loop (deposit, trade, buyback and burn, withdrawal and daily reconciliation) has been run on Solana devnet with public transactions. $SHELL launches on Raydium LaunchLab, from inside Shell City, and it is not tradable yet. Every city coin, from the businesses' coins to the ones players launch, is paired with $SHELL.

How do I take money out?

Earned $SHELL can leave once your Street Cred reaches 100. You build that up by playing over a few days: paid shifts, a meal or rent, a delivery or repair. You send it to any Solana address you choose, and the fee is shown before you confirm. The 2,500 $SHELL start grant is spendable in the game but never withdrawable.

Can I lose money?

Prices, fees and losses are always shown before you decide. Trading city coins can go down as well as up. Earnings are never guaranteed, and nothing on this site is financial advice.

Who is making it?

A small independent studio. Shell City is not made, sponsored or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
