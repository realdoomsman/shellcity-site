Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026. What we keep, why, and what we never do.

What we keep

Your Roblox user ID and display name; what you do in the game (shifts, purchases, progress); your balance and every ledger entry; your deposit address; the Solana addresses you withdraw to; and transaction signatures. Our servers talk to Roblox's game servers, not to your device, so we do not see your IP address.

Why

To run the game and your balance, pay withdrawals, stop fraud and farming, and answer support requests.

What is public

Solana is a public blockchain: deposits and withdrawals, with their addresses and amounts, can be seen by anyone, forever. We cannot remove them.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, show ads based on it, or ask for your seed phrase or private keys.

How long

The ledger is kept for good: it is append-only by design, so balances can always be checked. Other game data is kept while your account is active.

This website

shellcity.fun has no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

Contact

Questions or requests: @ShellCityFun on X.
