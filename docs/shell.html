How money works

$SHELL

Shell City's money is a Solana token. It is earned by working, spent at businesses run by the city and by players, and it can leave the game to an address you choose. Here is exactly how it moves.

The loop

Spent here,
paid here.

Nothing is minted to pay a wage. Money goes round: a player buys a burger, part of that pays the person who made it, and part of it makes the city's coins scarcer.

Every purchase

85% goes to the business. That fills its wage pot, and its workers are paid only from that pot. 10% goes to the business's coin buyback pool and 5% to the city treasury.

Buyback and burn

Once a coin's pool holds 10 $SHELL, the city buys that coin on chain and burns it. Busy businesses make their coin scarcer.

Pay by the stars

A task pays its posted price by your stars: three stars pay 100%, two pay 80%, one pays 30%. Daily pay tapers after your first 90 paid minutes.

One price source

Every price in the game comes from an on-chain pool. Our database only caches it, and it is never invented.

Own a business

Businesses can be claimed by players. The Burger Stand sells a meal for 1.50 $SHELL, and you can claim it for 150 $SHELL.

The start grant

New players get 2,500 $SHELL to get going. It is the only coin the game ever creates, it can be spent in the city, and it can never be withdrawn.

Taking it out

Built carefully

Boring on purpose.

Money code should be dull. Ours is a ledger you can't edit, checked against the chain every day.

Double-entry ledger

Every movement is a pair of entries that must sum to zero. Entries can be added but never edited or deleted, and amounts are whole base units: no floating point, ever.

Daily reconciliation

Once a day the ledger is compared with every token account the city controls, and the result is written down. Nothing is quietly corrected.

Final only

Deposits are credited, and withdrawals settled, only at Solana's finalized commitment. A withdrawal carries its own ID as the on-chain memo.

Hot and cold

The wallet that pays withdrawals holds only a working float. The treasury is a separate offline key.

A kill switch

Payouts can be paused with one setting. Requests still queue and nothing is lost.

Tested hard

More than 4,000 automated tests across the game and the backend, and every fix to a money path is proved by breaking it first.

Shell City holds players' balances for them (it is custodial): our backend keeps the keys, so players never have to. The code reviews so far have been our own, not an outside audit.
